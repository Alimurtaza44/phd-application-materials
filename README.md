## Abstracts and PDFs

### 1. M.Sc. thesis: neutron star–white dwarf binaries as multi-messenger sources
Population synthesis of NS–WD binaries with binary_c on the PARAM Rudra cluster, and their local merger-rate density. [Read the PDF](msc-thesis-nswd-binaries/Murtaza_MSc_thesis_NSWD_binaries.pdf) · [Folder with abstract](msc-thesis-nswd-binaries/)

### 2. Progenitor of SN 2023ixf from GROWTH-India Telescope light curves
Multi-band photometry of a nearby Type II supernova and MCMC fits of a shock-cooling model. [Read the PDF](sn2023ixf-shock-cooling/Murtaza_SN2023ixf_shock_cooling_report.pdf) · [Folder with abstract](sn2023ixf-shock-cooling/)

### 3. Accretion rate of the T Tauri star CVSO 109
Automated emission-line fitting of VLT/UVES spectra to measure mass accretion rates. [Read the PDF](prl-internship-cvso109-accretion/Murtaza_PRL_internship_report_CVSO109.pdf) · [Folder with abstract](prl-internship-cvso109-accretion/)

### 4. Shock-triggered star formation in FLASH
Hydrodynamic simulations of planar and supernova shocks hitting a molecular cloud. [Read the PDF](flash-shock-triggered-star-formation/Murtaza_FLASH_shock_triggered_star_formation.pdf) · [Folder with abstract](flash-shock-triggered-star-formation/)

### 5. LISA and LIGO noise curves
Analytic noise models and characteristic strains for gravitational-wave source classes. [Read the PDF](lisa-noise-curves/Murtaza_LISA_noise_curves_report.pdf) · [Folder with abstract](lisa-noise-curves/)
