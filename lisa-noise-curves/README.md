# LISA and LIGO noise curves and characteristic strains

Course project (PH-821), IIT Bombay, November 2025 · Gravitational-wave astronomy course of Prof. Archana Pai · Sole author

**PDF:** [Murtaza_LISA_noise_curves_report.pdf](Murtaza_LISA_noise_curves_report.pdf)

## Abstract

I implemented analytic noise power spectral densities for Advanced LIGO and for the Laser Interferometer Space Antenna (LISA) in Python, to compare the sensitivity windows of ground-based and space-based detectors. The LIGO model combines seismic, thermal and shot-noise terms. The LISA model follows Robson, Cornish and Liu (2019), with optical-metrology noise, acceleration noise, a galactic-binary confusion background, a transfer-function correction and an arm length of 2.5 × 10⁹ m. I compared the characteristic strain of different source classes (compact binary coalescences, continuous waves, stochastic backgrounds and bursts) with each detector's effective noise strain, √(f S_n), to show which sources fall in which band. LIGO is best matched to stellar-mass mergers near 100 Hz, while LISA reaches massive binaries but not typical Galactic pulsars. This is a course project that reproduces known sensitivity models and does not contain new astrophysical results.
